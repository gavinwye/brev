# Brev

A calm, distraction-free email client for Gmail. Native macOS app.

**Public alpha — free.** [Download the latest build »](https://github.com/gavinwye/brev/releases/latest)

- **Requirements:** macOS 14 or later · Apple Silicon (M-series).
- **Price:** free during the alpha. £29 one-time at public launch.
- **Website:** https://brev.gavinwye.com

## Install

1. Download `Brev-macOS-arm64.dmg` from the [latest release](https://github.com/gavinwye/brev/releases/latest).
2. Open the `.dmg` and drag **Brev** to Applications.
3. Launch it. The build is signed and notarized by Apple, so no Gatekeeper warning.

## Privacy

Brev talks only to Google (for your Gmail) and nothing else by default. It stores your mail in a
local cache on your Mac. There is no analytics or tracking in the alpha. Account questions:
gavin@gavinwye.com.
